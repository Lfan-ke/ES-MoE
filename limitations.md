# Known limitations

Scope of the evidence in `results/`, stated before anyone has to ask.

## Benchmark scope

- Numbers come from VisDrone2019-DET at imgsz 640, trained from scratch (no pretrained weights): a 25% subset for candidate selection, the full training set for confirmation. They are not COCO numbers and must not be read as a reproduction of the ES-MoE-N anchor (2.68M / 8.7 GFLOPs / 42.7 mAP), which is a COCO figure.
- Short budgets (tens of epochs from scratch) sit far from convergence. A gap measured here bounds the ranking of two blocks under equal budget; it does not predict the converged gap.
- The measured gain is **backbone-specific**, and the sign lines up with what the backbone ends in rather than with the release number: positive on the SPPF family (v5n, v8n, v9t), on zero once the end is an attention block (v10n, 11n), negative on 12n and 26n. Compatibility across seven generations is a statement about mechanics, not about accuracy; the earlier 20-epoch reading of 11n as "absent" did not survive the full budget, and the earlier "monotonic with generation" wording did not survive the seventh (see `docs/JUDGMENT.md`).
- The measured gain does not follow the schedule in one direction: paired mAP50 means are +0.0021 (3/3 seeds) at 20 epochs, +0.0013 (2/3) at 50 and +0.0046 (2/3) at 100, while the spread between seeds widens with the budget (+0.0104 to -0.0011 at 100 epochs). Treat the effect as small, positive on average and unreliable within a single run.
- Every number comes from one GPU. The DDP mechanics are verified (`scripts/verify.py`: the real worker file trains in a fresh interpreter; two gloo ranks each compute their own auxiliary term and their router gradients agree after all-reduce), but no multi-GPU accuracy figure exists.
- Sparse dispatch leaves the experts a batch did not route to out of the graph, so DDP needs `find_unused_parameters=True`. ultralytics builds it that way by default but switches it off under `compile=True`; the block cannot be used in that combination.

## Method scope

- `ESMoE` preserves channels by default (`c1 -> c1`). Upstream's `c1 -> c2` is supported through `out_channels=`, but a widened block cannot be grafted into a stock yaml: `parse_model` assumes `c2 == ch[f]` for third-party modules, so it has to be wired by hand.
- Channels are inferred on the first forward. A model that is scripted, exported, or `state_dict`-loaded before any forward has no expert weights to load yet.
- `attach_aux_loss` patches the task model class and keeps the weight at process scope, because the trainer rebuilds the model and takes the EMA copy before any callback runs. Consequently a process trains one aux-loss setting at a time, and a checkpoint reloaded in a process that never calls `attach_aux_loss` trains without the aux term.
- Export was verified against ONNX Runtime on inputs that route to different experts (`scripts/verify.py`); releases 0.1.0 to 0.1.2 baked the traced routing into the exported graph and should be re-exported.
- The load-balancing term is the Switch-Transformer formulation (`num_experts * sum(importance * load)`). No EMA normalisation of the aux magnitude is applied, unlike YOLO-Master's mixture controller.

## Protocol and evaluation

- The early records (imgsz 640, 20/50/100 epochs) do not sit under the repository's reproduction protocol and serve only same-budget comparison and parameter screening. The protocol-conformant matrix (imgsz 800, 120 epochs, `patience=0`, the full 548-image validation set; seven generations × three arms × three seeds and more) is complete, and conclusions are drawn from it. The default arm's sign lines up by what the backbone ends in — positive on the SPPF family, on zero once the end is an attention block, negative on the E2E head — and not by release number: v10n (−0.0002) sits below 11n (+0.0013), so the earlier "monotonic decay with generation" wording is withdrawn. Cell-by-cell verdicts in `docs/JUDGMENT.md`.
- Metrics in the run records come from the ultralytics evaluator with its default `max_det` of 300; `results/buckets.md` re-evaluates all 107 protocol-conformant checkpoints under COCO conventions with maxDets = 500. The two sets are not interchangeable, and neither is directly comparable to the official VisDrone leaderboard.
- The area buckets (small < 32², medium 32²–96², large ≥ 96², on ground-truth boxes in the original image) are the COCO definition adopted by this project, not a VisDrone one. On YOLOv8n, paired over three seeds: under the default wiring large objects get consistently worse (APl −0.0104, 0/3 wins) and small-object recall consistently better (ARs +0.0026, 3/3 wins); `rewire=True` turns APl positive (+0.0063, 2/3) and wins mAP50 on every seed, still by small margins. That layout is v8n-specific: on 26n the default wiring consistently loses small objects instead (APs −0.0045, 0/3) and on 12n the large-object direction flips with the seed. No fixed per-scale claim survives the four-generation matrix.

## Reporting

- **Two cells fall short of three seeds, and this declares them.** `yolov5n-e4k2w0.01-rewire` on the metax3.7 host reached seed 0 only (+0.0074); that host has been released and the cell cannot be completed. It supports the direction of the cross-host replication, enters no mean, and the same arm has three seeds on metax3.3. `yolov10n-e4k2w0.01-norm` has seed 0 only (+0.0001), a pilot run before the arm opened; the card time went elsewhere, and the conclusion about the output norm rests on the three seeds measured on v5n. Every other cell that enters a paired comparison has three.
- Seeds are reported individually and as mean ± sample standard deviation. With three seeds, the standard deviation is a coarse estimate; no significance test is claimed. On the full dataset one of the three seeds is effectively a tie (+0.0001 mAP50), so the effect is consistent in sign but not reliable within a single run.
- On top of the parameter increase the block costs about 5% wall-clock per epoch at imgsz 640 (829 s against 790 s, RTX 4090 D) and about 9% at imgsz 800 (85 against 78 minutes, RTX 4090).
- **Concentration does not predict accuracy; what the balancing term prevents is dead experts.** Over the 81 runs that have both a routing analysis and a paired delta, the leading expert's top-1 share (0.47–1.00) and the paired mAP50 delta (−0.0160 to +0.0109) correlate at **r = +0.044**, near nothing. The other layer of collapse is an expert that dies, in the top-2 on fewer than 1% of images. The Switch term at 0.01 leaves none in 66 checkpoints. All six checkpoints trained without a balancing term lose two experts, and five of six trained on a gate-reading objective (upstream's, and the paper's eq. 13) lose at least one, because a gate renormalised over the top-K has no gradient for an expert outside it. Removing the term moves accuracy inside the run-to-run spread: lower on 5 of 6 seeds, by −0.0050 and −0.0027 on average. The coefficient is computed by `scripts/routing.py --summarise` at the top of `results/routing.md`; the verdict is the sixth round of `docs/JUDGMENT.md`.
- **Two runs of one configuration differ by as much as the effect.** On the MetaX C500, repeating a configuration at the same seed moves mAP50 by 0.0045 on average and 0.0130 at worst after 120 epochs (five pairs); the same repeat on a 4090 reproduces bit for bit (one pair, 20 epochs). Six of the seven mean paired deltas sit inside that 0.0045; only v5n's +0.0055 clears it, and it is still well inside the largest gap of 0.0130. Conclusions here are therefore about **direction** and not magnitude. The table is at the end of `results/summary.md`.
- The metrics in a record come from the final re-validation of `best.pt`, not from the last row of `results.csv`, which is the last epoch. The two differ by 0.001 to 0.005, always in the same direction, because the best epoch is rarely the last one. Baseline and ESMoE arms take the same path, so paired deltas are unaffected.
- Paired deltas carry a 95% confidence interval as well as a mean. Three seeds leave two degrees of freedom and the interval is correspondingly wide: outside the shortest budget, **every interval straddles zero**. That is the precision three seeds support.
